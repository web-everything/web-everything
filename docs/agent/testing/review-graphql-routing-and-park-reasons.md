## Review GraphQL routing and park reasons

The gh shim embeds `ownerFromGhArgv.toString()`: keep its dependencies limited to
helpers already embedded by the shim. GraphQL routing requires separate owner and
name/repo fields; query text alone never identifies the installation.

Unattended confirm holds persist `acceptHoldReason` on the run, sanitized before
storage and again when projecting historical evidence. Redact credentials before
the 300-character bound. Exhausted no-verdict holds reuse the referral needs-you
claim ledger, keyed by head and run-count. Legacy claims without a count allow one
new alert for the current count. Landed verdicts and cool-down holds do not alert.
Keep CI-held record preflight refusals read-only; they are not confirm parks.

The needs-you feed also keys this alert kind by `reviewRunCount`; other kinds and
legacy events keep their keys. Its display reason is capped at 100 characters,
so the structured `lastParkReason` field carries the full bounded diagnostic.
