The plateau-app "no backend" rule is a phase constraint, not a permanent prohibition. Phases: (1) now = browser-only, no backend, flat cost; (2) a hosted multi-product suite, parked; (3) later = paid, backend-backed function, deferred behind phase 1.

**How to apply:** when a backend, hosted or live-serve idea surfaces, it is out of phase, not forbidden. Think it through and capture it (park it under the suite card or a new `status: parked` item with `dateParked` and an "unpark when" note). Never silently drop it. Never build it before live-serve is on the roadmap. Codified in plateau-app/CLAUDE.md ("THE PHASE RULE"). Relates to [[project_linear_cost_revenue_on_device]].
