# we: review stringency slice 2 — settings table used by the ruling step

Add the review-stringency settings table (class by level to block, expedite, debt or advisory), every cell a cascade setting with its source logged, and make the ruling step read it. Fail closed on gaps. Current level internal delivery.

## Input contract

Implementation wire domains (exact ASCII tokens, no coercion): `severity` is `low | medium | high | critical`; the security high row means `high | critical`, and lower means `low | medium`. `confidence` is `low | medium | high`; only the exact token `low` triggers the confidence cap (no numeric threshold or string-to-number conversion). Slice 1 owns the exact closed `class` tokens and their ratified table-row mapping; each token maps to exactly one row, with security additionally keyed by severity. Level tokens are `internal-delivery | dogfood | public-preview | public-release`, in that order. Outcome tokens are `block | expedite | debt | advisory`.

`debtQuadrant` belongs to debt admission, not every reviewer finding. Its exact domain is `prudent-deliberate | prudent-inadvertent | reckless-deliberate | reckless-inadvertent`. Apply this check only when the effective outcome after classification and the confidence cap is debt: only explicit `prudent-deliberate` permits admission; every other, missing or malformed value blocks that proposed deferral. The accountable debt owner supplies the assessment and rationale for the proposed deferral, confirmed by the authenticated operator through the protected writer; a reviewer cannot infer the defect author's intent or grant admission by labelling it. Block, expedite and advisory outcomes do not require or consult this field. Overdue expedite still creates a due-now gate obligation without a quadrant assessment; it is not admission of ordinary deferred debt and cannot be dropped for lacking a quadrant (slice 3 A2/A8).

Matching path/pattern rules outrank the reviewer tag. Multiple matches selecting the same class deduplicate; different selected classes are an ambiguous policy and block before the confidence cap, independent of rule kind, order or confidence. Never select the last iterated rule. Invalid reviewer fields still block before overrides.

## Non-goals

- [N1] Debt lifecycle, panel rendering and advisory jobs belong to slices 3–5; this slice does not change ratified cells.

## Edge cases this change must handle

One line per class: either the handling, or `n/a: <why>`.

1. **Untrusted text** — A8 validates every model-supplied class, severity and confidence field before policy evaluation; source code, docs, config and data cannot supply policy or authority (A10).
2. **Truncated reads** — A9 blocks on any incomplete or unreadable finding or settings snapshot.
3. **Shared state files** — A11 uses atomic replacement and a revision check; no mixed or stale snapshot is effective.
4. **Fail closed** — A1, A6–A9 reject invalid tables and consumed fields; always-block survives failed second checks (A12). Only an effective debt outcome invokes the admission guard (A5); other outcomes remain reachable (A18).
5. **Identity scoping** — A10 binds authenticated review evidence to the repository, exact head/base and approved diff, never a self-declared role.
6. **State over time** — stale approvals and concurrent writes refuse under A10–A11; only a fresh approved revision becomes effective.
7. **Who wrote it** — protected CI verifies the GitHub review actor against the protected operator allowlist (A10); agent proposals cannot change effective settings (A4).

## Risks

The wrong security row, confidence default or ambiguous override could weaken outcomes (A2–A3, A5, A8, A12–A13). File-only reads could bypass cascade provenance (A14), and unpinned initial settings could silently start at the wrong level or disable lessons (A17). Applying debt admission to every finding could erase expedite/advisory outcomes (A5, A18). Relaxed levels and missing state could permit delivery (A1, A6–A9). Forged identity, PR-edited trust roots and races could authorize unsafe writes (A4, A10–A11, A15); oversized or flooded inputs could exhaust ingress (A8, A16).
