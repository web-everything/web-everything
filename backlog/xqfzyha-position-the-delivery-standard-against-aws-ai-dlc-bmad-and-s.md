# Position the delivery standard against AWS AI-DLC, BMAD and Spec Kit: contract layer or another method

Source: AI Delivery Landscape research brief, 2026-10-08, sections 'Open decisions' item 5 and 'Closest existing efforts'. Competing as another delivery method means fighting established adoption (AWS AI-DLC Workflows v2 is roughly 60% of the scope, Spec Kit ~115k stars). Being the conformance-tested contract layer those methods run on makes them adopters: they map their stages and agents onto the role set and protocols. Brief's recommendation: contract layer. Operator's call; not ruled here.
