## Collision check (web search, 2026-11-17, not legal clearance)

- Longshore: no AI-delivery product of that name found among comparable tools (Forge, Apex, Shipwright, Shift AX, Loom). The ShoreAI name with Shore GmbH risk was avoided. Common English word, so a trademark search in classes 9 and 42 is still owed.
- Ship Evermore / shipevermore: no hit. The EVERMORE word mark is crowded (Evermore Software, Evermore Technology, Soda Health, and others, in classes 9, 36, 42), as the 8 Oct screen said. Risk stays moderate. "Ship" plus a descriptive standard use may help, but only a formal search says.
- Platform Forever / everstandards: no hit. Nearest is an unrelated Evernode Standards repo (EVS numbering); different field.
- Domains: from the 8 Oct brief, platformforever.dev and shipevermore.dev were free; not re-checked offline today.
- Package names (npm, crates, PyPI) are not checked; no search tool for registries here. Verify when the first package is published.

## Verdict

Nothing is left to fork. The choice was made in chat with reasons logged. The only open items are validation steps: a formal trademark search (#186) before public use, and the everstandards docs-hub role, which is a support-both, not a fork. Prepared as a ratify-the-existing-choice item.
